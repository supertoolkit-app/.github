1. PDF Assembly, Organization, Page Formatting & Pagination

Preparing court filings, board packets, university theses, or client proposals requires combining disparate files, fixing upside-down scans, trimming scanner borders, and stamping consistent page numbers:

PDF Merger & Combiner

Join unlimited PDF documents into one unified file. Drag and drop files to rearrange their exact sequence, inspect page counts before compiling, and download the combined PDF instantaneously with no file-count or megabyte caps.

Route: supertoolkit.app/pdf-merger/

PDF Page Organizer & Splitter

Extract specific page ranges (e.g., 1-5, 8, 12-15), delete unwanted blank pages, reorder sheets visually, or burst a multi-page PDF into individual single-page PDF files bundled neatly in a local ZIP archive.

Route: supertoolkit.app/pdf-splitter/

Add Page Numbers to PDF (Bates Numbering)

Stamp clean pagination across legal exhibits and reports. Choose from standard formats (Page 1 of 24, 1 / 24), Roman numerals, or custom legal Bates prefixes (e.g., EXHIBIT-A-0001) with full control over header/footer position, margin offsets, and typography.

Route: supertoolkit.app/page-numbers-pdf/

Rotate PDF Pages Permanently

Most PDF viewers only rotate a page temporarily while you view it. This tool rewrites the PDF page rotation dictionary (90°, 180°, or 270° clockwise) across all pages, odd/even pages, or individual sheets and saves the corrected orientation permanently.

Route: supertoolkit.app/rotate-pdf/

Crop PDF Margins & Bounding Box

Remove dark scanner edges, printer crop marks, or oversized white margins by adjusting the PDF MediaBox and CropBox coordinates in points or millimeters across the entire document.

Route: supertoolkit.app/crop-pdf/

Watermark PDF Stamper Studio

Protect intellectual property and draft proposals by stamping diagonal or horizontal watermarks (such as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or DO NOT DISTRIBUTE) with customizable opacity, hex color, rotation angle, and font size.

Route: supertoolkit.app/watermark-pdf/

3. PDF Conversion & Content Extraction (Word DOCX, Images & Plaintext)

Need to edit a read-only PDF in Microsoft Word, turn phone camera scans into a clean PDF packet, or pull high-resolution figures out of a research paper?

PDF to Word (DOCX) Converter

Reconstructs read-only PDF documents into native, editable Microsoft Word (.docx) files. Preserves document headings, paragraph grouping, bullet lists, and typography so you can edit contracts and resumes in Word or Google Docs.

Images to PDF Converter

Combine JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG, AVIF, and TIFF photos into a single PDF document. Supports custom page sizes (A4, US Letter, Fit-to-Image), portrait/landscape orientation, margin padding, and custom output filenames.

Extract Images from PDF Document

Extracts embedded photos, charts, architectural diagrams, and scanned pages from any PDF into crisp, lossless PNG image files with 1-click individual or batch downloads.

PDF to Text Plaintext Extractor

Parses text streams across all pages of a PDF and outputs clean, unformatted plain text (.txt) with instant clipboard copy, word/character counts, and zero layout clutter.
