Frequently Asked Questions (Audio & Video DSP)

Does Super ToolKit reduce audio quality or add watermarks?

Never. All internal signal processing runs in uncompressed 32-bit floating-point PCM inside the browser's Web Audio API graph. You can export either bit-perfect uncompressed WAV or high-bitrate 320kbps MP3 with zero audio watermarks and zero duration limits.

Why is Super ToolKit faster than 123apps or Convertio for large video files?

Legacy converters upload your entire video file over the internet to a remote server before extracting the audio. Super ToolKit's Video to Audio Converter reads the file directly from your local SSD/RAM, meaning a 2GB video begins converting the instant you select it.
